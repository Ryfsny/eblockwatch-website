eblockwatch FAQ

Questions people actually ask, answered the same way as on WhatsApp. If you would rather talk it through, send Hi to 067 380 5529.

What is eblockwatch?

eblockwatch is a human-based network powered by technology, not a product and not a group. Since 2001 Andre Snyman has built it one relationship at a time, and it is growing to manage about 250,000 people through almost one cell phone. When you join, Andre has your number and you have his. Every member gets the BackApp — that is your connection to him. We look after you between safe zones — home, work, school — so that if something goes wrong, Andre can call the right people.

The BackApp is a support and crisis-management network. It is not a panic button, and it is not an armed-response service.

How do I join?

You can join in about a minute, for free, on WhatsApp: send Hi to 067 380 5529, or start on this site and confirm on WhatsApp. Tell us your first name and surname, your suburb for home, and the name and number of the person we should call if we can't reach you. Then get the BackApp — every member gets it, and that is how you connect to Andre.

If you already signed up on the website, stay on the WhatsApp chat — it is the same eblockwatch — and we will get you onto the next step, including the BackApp.

If you scanned a sticker and you are not registered yet, you cannot contact Andre directly. Join first, then get the BackApp. A sticker on its own is not membership.

Do you cover my town?

Yes. eblockwatch is not a local patrol. Members are in towns across the country, and you can join from wherever you are.

Is this a WhatsApp group?

No. You don't join a group. You join Andre. The WhatsApp chat is your line to him, once you are registered, and the BackApp is your connection to him. You can add the people you care about as the ones we call first, and a Family membership is R250, for a family of five.

What does it cost?

Single membership is R150. Someone knows when you should arrive, and you get more of Andre for crisis management.

Family membership is R250, for a family of five. You, your ICE contacts, and those family members get more of Andre for crisis management.

Payment is through your own Paystack link.

If you want to go ahead, WhatsApp will send you your own payment link. Don't use an old shop link, or a link meant for someone else. To change a plan, or if a payment is not showing, talk to Andre on the same chat — a second link can charge you twice.

What is the Cyberdome?

Your Cyberdome is simply what we already hold so we don't ask for forms on a bad day: who you are, where's home, who we call first, and your car if you have one. You don't need a car to be a member. Every detail you add makes help faster. You can save as many safe zones as you like — home, a second home, work, school, the gym, a parent's house.

What is the BackApp?

The BackApp is free for every member — entry level, Single, and Family. It is your connection to Andre. It quietly notices when you leave a safe zone, and it helps us know when you should arrive. Today, trips and help happen on WhatsApp. If you are registered and you shake your phone, Andre is alerted and you are triaged into the situation room and the control room.

It is a support and crisis-management network, not a panic button and not armed response. Location is shared only if you have switched it on in your own app.

A few simple tiles are coming; they are not live yet.

iPhone installs through Apple TestFlight. Android: we send your download link on WhatsApp. In the iPhone app we do not talk about payment or upgrades — membership is handled on WhatsApp and on this website.

How do trips work, including at Christmas?

Tell the WhatsApp chat where you are going and when you should arrive. If you are sharing a Waze drive, we can use that time. When you get there, send ARRIVED or SAFE. If that time passes and we haven't heard from you, we ask you first.

You do not have to wait for the BackApp before you travel. You can start a trip on WhatsApp. The BackApp is still how members connect to Andre, so we will help you finish setting it up.

Nobody is watching a live map of you — not us, not the media, not the police, and not other members. The point is simpler: someone knows when you should arrive. When you arrive, the trip closes. We do not keep tracking you afterwards.

For a long drive with overnight stops, send ARRIVED at each stop, then start the next leg when you leave.

If your family is in another car, or at home while you travel, we can look after them once they are on your family circle. They will also get the BackApp.

A designated-trip tile is coming. It is not live yet. Today, the person travelling should start the trip on WhatsApp.

What is Cop on eblock?

Cop on eblock is how eblockwatch looks after you at a roadblock. You get a sticker for your windscreen, a short voice note, and, if you are a registered member, a way to let Andre know you are at a stop. The sticker is a deterrent: it shows you are part of eblockwatch and that you have the connectivity to protect you against abusive behaviour, from whoever. Registration is what matters, and the membership is the real value.

At a roadblock: pull over where they show you, stay calm, and follow the officers' instructions. Never offer or pay a bribe. If you can, play the short voice note: Good day, and say e-blockwatch clearly. Your sticker already says recorded for everyone's safety. Stay on the WhatsApp chat. When you drive off, say you're OK. If you're not OK, send 9 or HELP.

How do I get and use the sticker?

Stickers come with Single or Family membership. Print as many as you like for your own vehicles — not to give out to other people. They are a download. Print them full size at PostNet and put them next to the licence disc, so they are visible at a stop.

The wording is: recorded for everyone's safety. The sticker is a deterrent: it shows you are part of eblockwatch and that you have the connectivity to protect you against abusive behaviour, from whoever. It does not mean a camera is live-streaming you, and it does not mean police or the media are watching. Registration is what matters, and the membership is the real value.

A sticker alone does not register you with Andre. If you scan the sticker QR and you are not registered yet, you will land on the eblockwatch WhatsApp chat, and we will help you join and get the BackApp — that is how members connect to Andre. You cannot contact Andre directly with only a sticker. If you are already a member, tell us if you are at a roadblock, or what you need.

What happens if I shake my phone?

If you are a registered member, a shake alerts Andre and you are triaged into the situation room and the control room. It does not alert a group, and we do not record officers. Location is only shared if you have switched it on in your own app. We do not track you after a stop closes.

If you are not registered yet, a shake or a scanned sticker is not a direct line to Andre. Join first and get the BackApp. If you are not OK, send 9 or HELP — a crisis is never refused, and there is no charge.

What if I get into a situation?

Stay as safe as you can. If you can use WhatsApp, tell the chat what is happening, in your own words. Send a pin if you can.

If it is urgent, type 9 or HELP. A crisis is never refused, and there is no charge, and you will not be left waiting on a menu.

If you are a registered Single or Family member, or one of their ICE or family members, Arnie triages you through to Andre, into the situation room and the control room. If you are an entry-level member, the people you named as emergency contacts are reached first, and Andre is backup. If you are not registered yet, we will help you and get you registered onto the BackApp — that is the connection to Andre.

Tiles for "stuck on the side of the road", "out of fuel", a good Samaritan stop, Cop on eblock, and a designated trip are coming; they are not live yet. Tapping a tile will pull your car and contacts from your Cyberdome, and evidence will come to WhatsApp.

How do I change my details, or log in?
