Cop on eblock

How eblockwatch looks after you at a roadblock.

Cop on eblock gives you a sticker for your windscreen, a short voice note, and, if you are a registered member, a way to let Andre know you are at a stop. The sticker is a deterrent: it shows you are part of eblockwatch and that you have the connectivity to protect you against abusive behaviour, from whoever. Registration is what matters, and the membership is the real value.

At a roadblock

  1. Pull over where they show you, and stay calm.
  2. Follow the officers' instructions. Never offer or pay a bribe.
  3. If you can, play the short voice note: "Good day", with e-blockwatch said clearly.
  4. Send ROADBLOCK on the WhatsApp chat and Andre knows you are at a stop.
  5. When you drive off, send OK. If you are not OK, send 9 or HELP. A crisis is never refused, and there is no charge.

What it is not

We do not ask you to record officers. Your location is shared only if you switched it on in your own app, and nobody tracks you after the stop. The sticker reads "recorded for everyone's safety". It does not mean a camera is live-streaming you, or that police or the media are watching.

The sticker

Stickers come with Single (R150) or Family (R250, for a family of five) membership. Print as many as you like for your own vehicles, full size at PostNet, and put them next to the licence disc. They are not for handing out to other people. A sticker on its own does not register you with Andre: join first, then get the BackApp.
