What I have learnt

Crime here runs on surprise. Take that away and they lose.

Surprise is their weapon

An attack at a robot is over in seconds. Awareness before the crime matters more than any button pressed after it.

Pressing a panic button after an incident is like moaning at the ref after the game.

The cameras watch. Nobody connects them.

We spend billions on cameras, patrols, apps and panic buttons, and still get robbed at the same robots. The missing link is a person who sees it and tells one hub, straight away.

Intelligence comes from people

Every report is a piece of the puzzle. We put the pieces together, then put the boots behind the intelligence. Without your eyes, the response works blind.
