eblockwatch Privacy

eBlockwatch (Pty) Ltd, reg. 2002/000418/07, Johannesburg. Updated 12 November 2026.

Your data, your decision.

Log in at www.eblockwatch.co.za to see everything we hold about you and what we do with it.

What we hold

Your name, WhatsApp number and email; your home pin and safe zones; your emergency contact; your car description; your trip locations while a trip runs or the BackApp is on; your membership payments (Paystack handles cards; we never see them).

Why

To look after you between safe zones and to know who to call when something goes wrong.

Who sees it

Andre Snyman and the eblockwatch team. Your emergency contact and responders only when you need help. Our service providers (Meta/WhatsApp, Supabase, Paystack) only to run the service. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Delete your data

Unsubscribe in the BackApp or on your member page, and your data deletes immediately. Or WhatsApp Andre Snyman on +27 82 561 1065 and he deletes it for you. We keep only payment records, because the law requires it.

Keeping it

We keep your data only while you are a member.
